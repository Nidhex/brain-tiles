Brain Tiles Guide & Rules

🎯 Objective

Slide the numbered tiles on the 4×4 grid. When two tiles with the same number collide during a move, they fuse into one tile with the sum of their values (e.g. 2 + 2 = 4, 4 + 4 = 8, ..., 1024 + 1024 = 2048).

🕹️ Controls

  • Desktop: Press ↑ ↓ ← → or W A S D.
  • Mobile: Swipe across the board in any of the 4 directions.
  • D-Pad: Tap the navigation buttons below the board on mobile.

🧠 Pro Tips

  • Keep your highest value tile anchored in one of the corners (like the bottom-right).
  • Build a cascading snake pattern: arrange descending numbers sequentially in the row containing your highest tile.
  • Use the Undo button if you make an accidental move!
